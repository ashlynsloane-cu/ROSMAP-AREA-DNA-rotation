ROSMAP AREA PROJECT — STAGE 10E PHENOTYPE-DESIGN ROBUSTNESS FREEZE
Date: 2026-09-28

====================================================================================================
WHAT STAGE 10E IS
====================================================================================================

Stage 10E is NOT a new direct confirmation of Stage 9.

The stages answer different questions:

Stage 9:
    Empirical/descriptive.
    What signal geometry do real ROSMAP AREA-predominant features show?
    Stage 9 phenotype generalization already tested whether the lower-overlap AREA geometry
    generalized across NCI/MCI/AD, Braak, and CERAD contrasts.

Stage 10A-D:
    Mechanistic simulation.
    Under known ground truth, which signal geometries favor AREA, DESeq2, limma, or neither?

Stage 10E:
    Robustness of Stage 10.
    Do the Stage 10A-D method-behavior patterns persist when the real sample size, group balance,
    covariates, and reference-group distribution are changed to other pre-specified ROSMAP
    phenotype contrasts?

Therefore Stage 10E bridges Stage 9 and Stage 10, but it should not be described as an independent
biological replication of Stage 9.

The five phenotype contrasts are correlated and share participants. They are robustness conditions,
not five independent biological replications.

====================================================================================================
CONTRAST-SPECIFIC CALIBRATION
====================================================================================================

Each Stage 10E contrast is calibrated from its own real reference group.

Examples:
    NCI -> MCI:
        calibration reference = NCI

    MCI -> AD:
        calibration reference = MCI

    Braak 0-2 -> 3-4:
        calibration reference = Braak 0-2

    Braak 3-4 -> 5-6:
        calibration reference = Braak 3-4

    CERAD 3-4 -> 1-2:
        calibration reference = CERAD 3-4, the less-pathological group

This allows Stage 10E to test the actual phenotype-specific sample size, group imbalance,
covariate design, and baseline distribution rather than simply relabeling the NCI-vs-AD
simulation.

====================================================================================================
NON-NULL BURDEN / BH DESIGN
====================================================================================================

Stage 10A-D production used:
    101 scenarios x 4 signal features/scenario = 404 signal features per replicate.

A naive Stage 10E reduction to:
    12 scenarios x 4 features = 48 signal features

would materially change the genome-wide BH environment and make power comparisons less comparable.

Therefore frozen Stage 10E production uses:
    12 scenarios x 34 signal features/scenario = 408 signal features per replicate.

This approximately preserves the Stage 10A-D non-null burden while reducing the number of distinct
scenario cells.

This decision is made BEFORE Stage 10E outcomes are inspected.

====================================================================================================
SIMULATION MECHANICS
====================================================================================================

Stage 10E inherits the frozen Stage 10 v5 mechanics.

RNA:
    negative-binomial count simulation
    contrast-specific empirical reference-group calibration
    real size-factor heterogeneity
    effect sizes calibrated from empirical reference-group log2(normalized + 0.5) SD
    VARIANCE_ONLY:
        finite-sample arithmetic-mean-preserving multiplicative heterogeneity
    SYMMETRIC_TWO_TAIL:
        realized carrier arithmetic-mean balancing before anchored stochastic perturbation

TMT:
    log2 abundance scale
    primary baseline generator:
        empirical centered residual bootstrap from the contrast-specific reference group
    frozen global-complete universe:
        5,211 proteins

Methods:
    RNA:
        Regular AREA vs DESeq2

    TMT:
        Regular AREA vs limma

AREA inference:
    FWL partial rank
    residual-df-corrected analytic variance
    Gaussian two-sided p
    BH
    no block

====================================================================================================
PRIMARY QUESTIONS
====================================================================================================

For each modality and contrast:

1. Does AREA remain calibrated?
2. Does the conventional method remain calibrated?
3. Does broad directional signal remain detectable?
4. Do low/intermediate-penetrance subset and one-tail effects continue to favor the same method?
5. Does symmetric two-tail spreading remain largely invisible to AREA?
6. Does the RNA variance-only AREA advantage persist outside NCI-vs-AD?
7. Do apparent method differences change substantially when phenotype-specific sample size,
   group imbalance, and reference-group distribution change?
8. Does the monocyte DESeq2 elevated realized-FDR behavior persist across phenotype designs?

Interpretation should emphasize qualitative robustness, not treat each phenotype as an independent
replication.

====================================================================================================
WHAT STAGE 10E DOES NOT TEST
====================================================================================================

Stage 10E does NOT directly test whether the real Stage 9 AREA-predominant genes are generated by
one exact simulation mechanism.

Stage 10E does NOT replace Stage 9 phenotype generalization.

Stage 10E does NOT establish that AREA is universally superior or inferior.

Stage 10E does NOT make the five phenotype axes independent.

Stage 10E does NOT alter the Stage 10A-D frozen primary results.

====================================================================================================
FREEZE RULE
====================================================================================================

The 12-scenario panel, 34 signal features/scenario, five contrasts, reference/case orientation,
feature universes, covariates, seed policy, and primary methods are frozen before Stage 10E
outcomes are inspected.

Any later deviation must be labeled explicitly as a sensitivity or follow-up analysis.
