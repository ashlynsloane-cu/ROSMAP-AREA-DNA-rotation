PRIMARY SCIENTIFIC QUESTION
====================================================================================================

Stage 10 asks:

    Under which pre-specified signal geometries does covariate-adjusted Regular AREA recover
    true signal better, similarly, or worse than conventional mean-based methods, and which
    mechanisms can reproduce the distributional geometry observed empirically in Stage 9?

The purpose is mechanistic comparison under known ground truth.

Stage 10 is NOT designed to:
    - search for simulation regimes that make AREA look favorable
    - tune the scenario grid after viewing power results
    - establish global superiority of AREA
    - treat the three omic modalities as interchangeable

Stage 9 motivates the geometry question but Stage 10 production outcomes may not alter the frozen
simulation design.

FROZEN EFFECT CALIBRATION
====================================================================================================

RNA:
    Effect magnitude is calibrated using empirical NCI
        SD(log2(normalized_count + 0.5))

    Signals are injected on the linear expected-count scale.

TMT:
    Effect magnitude is calibrated using empirical NCI SD on the existing TMT log2 abundance scale.

Signal direction:
    Directional scenarios randomize direction by simulated feature so that the simulation does not
    create an artificial one-sided advantage.

FROZEN RNA GENERATION RULES
====================================================================================================

Baseline RNA generation:
    Negative-binomial counts calibrated to real ROSMAP NCI data.

Calibration includes:
    - real-data baseline expression means
    - negative-binomial dispersion
    - empirical library-size / size-factor heterogeneity
    - empirical NCI log2-expression SD
    - real frozen sample size and covariate design

Known limitation:
    Simulated genes are marginally calibrated and independent.
    Gene-gene co-expression is not reproduced in the primary Stage 10 simulation.
    This limitation is frozen and must not be changed after inspecting production results.


SYMMETRIC_TWO_TAIL technical definition:
    Realized normalized-expression anchors are used and carrier multipliers are explicitly
    rebalanced so that the carrier arithmetic mean is preserved exactly BEFORE the anchored
    stochastic Poisson perturbation.

Pilot audit:
    Monocyte max |expected/reference mean ratio - 1|:
        4.441e-16

    DLPFC max |expected/reference mean ratio - 1|:
        5.551e-16


VARIANCE_ONLY technical definition:
    v5 draws lognormal multiplicative heterogeneity and then divides the realized AD multipliers
    by their arithmetic mean.

    Therefore:
        mean(multiplier_AD) = 1 for every simulated RNA feature, up to floating-point precision.

    This normalization is only a constant shift on the log2 scale, so it does not change the
    realized log2-scale SD of the perturbation.

Pilot audit after v5 correction:
    Monocyte max |mean_multiplier_AD - 1|:
        2.220446049250313e-16

    DLPFC max |mean_multiplier_AD - 1|:
        2.220446049250313e-16

This v5 correction was made BEFORE the Stage 10 production freeze because the pilot technical audit
showed that expectation-only centering could create substantial finite-sample mean shifts.
The correction was motivated by scenario-definition fidelity, not by comparative method power.

FROZEN TMT GENERATION RULES
====================================================================================================

Primary TMT baseline generator:
    empirical_bootstrap

Construction:
    For each globally complete real TMT protein:
        1. use the real NCI participants
        2. subtract that protein's NCI mean
        3. retain the resulting centered empirical residual distribution
        4. bootstrap those empirical centered residuals with replacement
        5. add the selected template protein's real NCI mean

Purpose:
    Preserve empirical marginal skew, heavy tails, and non-Gaussian distribution shape better than
    a Normal(mean, SD) generator.

Primary production:
    empirical_bootstrap

Pre-specified sensitivity:
    gaussian

The Gaussian TMT generator may be run as a sensitivity analysis but may not replace the empirical
bootstrap as the primary analysis after production results are inspected.
